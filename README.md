Nazaret Valero – Virtual Assistant & Web Design

About the Project

A professional website showcasing remote administrative assistance and web design services for contractors, construction companies, and small businesses.

The platform combines administrative support and digital solutions to help businesses improve their organization, streamline daily operations, and strengthen their online presence.

Services

- Quotation and budget preparation.
- Data entry and Excel management.
- Document organization and management.
- Email and WhatsApp assistance.
- Calendar and appointment management.
- Website design and digital solutions.

Website Sections

- Main: Introduction and main services.
- About Me: Professional background and experience.
- Services: Overview of available services.
- Projects: Portfolio of administrative documents and websites.
- Free Estimate: Estimate requests and web consultations.
- Contact: Contact information and communication form.

Design & Visual Identity

A clean, elegant, and professional interface featuring:

- Navy blue, gold, white, warm ivory, and blue-gray color palette.
- Elegant typography and organized layouts.
